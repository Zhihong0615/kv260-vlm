# RM10 prior-art boundary (narrow scan)

**Scope:** FPGA floating-point GEMM/reduction recurrence, partial sums/reduction trees, and edge ViT/VLM FFN accelerators. This is a bounded prior-art check, not a proof of absence.

## Findings

- **Interleaved independent accumulators are established prior art.** Marongiu & Palazzari (ISC 2020) explicitly replace a recurrence-bound scalar product with `L` independent partial scalar products, where `L` is the pipelined MADD latency, then externally reduce those partials. Their Arria 10 example uses 32 single-precision adders and 32 multipliers. Their text explicitly sets aside finite-precision associativity effects, so it is precedent for the architecture, not evidence that a new RM10 ordering passes its numerical gate. AMD's Vitis HLS documentation independently gives an interleaved-channel accumulator example and reports standard-precision accumulation II typically 3–5 on non-Versal devices; its low-precision accumulator reaches II=1 at roughly 3x resources and may mismatch C simulation.
- **Split partial reductions and pipelined trees are established too.** Zhuo, Morris & Prasanna (TPDS 2007) analyze tree-traversal and striding reduction circuits for sequential FP streams, with trade-offs in adders, buffers and latency. Sun & Zambreno (FPT 2009) likewise describe an FP adder accumulator using partial sums and log-sum reduction; they note the reordered sum can change result accuracy. Therefore A/B/C are implementation candidates, not standalone novelty claims.
- **ViT FFN/GEMM and fabric pressure already appear in FPGA work, but with different arithmetic/workloads.** Edge-MoE (ICCAD 2023) reports an end-to-end HLS FPGA accelerator for multi-task ViT and specifically notes softmax/GELU can consume over half the LUT resource. FlexViT (FPL 2026) maps fully connected and convolutional ViT layers through an INT8 GEMM engine on PYNQ-Z2, reporting resource use and model-level CPU-relative speedups. These establish edge-ViT and LUT-pressure precedent; neither is evidence for F16 weight × F32 activation → F32 reduction on KV260 or a VLM vision FFN recurrence wall.
- **No directly matching primary source was identified in this bounded search** for the combination of exact RM10 arithmetic semantics, resource-constrained edge FPGA, measured CLB/routing-limited recurrence-decoupling, and full MiniCPM-V validation. Treat this as a search result, not a novelty proof. A 2026 FCCM paper titled *ReCoVLM* is a relevant VLM follow-up lead (DOI below); its full text was not inspected here, so it is excluded from the matched-work conclusion.

## Novelty boundary for RM10

Do not claim novelty for multi-accumulator interleaving, K partitioning, or reduction trees by themselves. If the board and full-request results pass, the defensible contribution may be the **measured workload-specific mapping and resource boundary**: whether recurrence decoupling on K26 converts fabric/adder cost into sustained vision-FFN throughput while preserving the frozen F16×F32→F32 numerical gate, and whether that gain survives full MiniCPM-V execution. The claimed CLB/routing wall must be supported by RM10 HLS/Vivado reports; prior ViT work already discusses LUT pressure in other operators.
